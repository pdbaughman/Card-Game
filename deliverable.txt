NOTE: Matching cards means matching categories, not exact duplicate cards. 
i.e. a card that has a banana on it will have another card that has a fruit on it such as an apple.

Here is a list of requirements that our project must have:

1. A main menu with appropriate buttons (start, options (if applicable), and exit
2. scene with at least 8 cards
3. cards can flip over when clicked
4. when clicking two matching cards in a row, they permanently flip face up
5. cards are randomized but each card will always have a corresponding matching card
